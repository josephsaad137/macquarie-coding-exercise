Coding Exercise: Market Data Quality &amp;

Analytics Platform

Objective
Design and implement a business-facing application that ingests historical futures market
data, generates analytics and identify data quality issues.

Context
Your team provides futures market data to traders, risk managers, and analysts. Data may
contain missing records, duplicates, invalid values, or timestamp issues. Business users
require a simple application to understand data quality and derive market insights.
Example data is here - lynx1231/historical-futures-data-sample at main

Data Ingestion
 Accept CSV or Parquet files
 Process futures market data including Contract, Timestamp, Open, High, Low, Close and
Volume
 Handle missing values, duplicates and malformed records

Market Data Analytics
 Generate daily OHLCV bars
 Calculate rolling 15-minute VWAP
 Allow filtering by contract and date range

Data Quality Validation
 Detect missing timestamps and data gaps
 Identify duplicate records
 Flag invalid prices or volumes
 Optionally identify statistical outliers

Intelligent Insights
 Identify recurring data quality patterns
 Suggest cleansing or validation rules
 Rule-based or AI-assisted approaches are acceptable

Technical Expectations
 Clean architecture
 Extensible design
 Good test coverage
 Thoughtful handling of edge cases

Deliverables
 Working solution
 README
 Architecture overview
 UI/dashboard
 Unit and integration tests
